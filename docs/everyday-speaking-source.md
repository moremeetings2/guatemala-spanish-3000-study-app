- Eighteen prompts lack a Spanish answer in the transcript. Their translations were supplied during editorial cleanup, listed below.

- Source English prompts are preserved. Clear spelling, grammar, punctuation, and translation mismatches were corrected in Spanish. No audio from the video is bundled; Listen uses the device's Spanish speech synthesis and the learner's voice/speed settings.
